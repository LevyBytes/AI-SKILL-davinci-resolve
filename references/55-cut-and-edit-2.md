# Cut And Edit (part 2)

> Davinci Resolve reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

With DaVinci Resolve 21, the Source Tape Viewer on the Edit page can finally handle Stereoscopic 3D footage as well as Multicam clips.

### Subtitle Fade Controls

In DaVinci Resolve 21, subtitle clips pick up the same kind of fade-in and fade-out handles already found on video and audio clips. Grab a fade handle at either edge of the clip and drag it to dial in how long that fade should be.
